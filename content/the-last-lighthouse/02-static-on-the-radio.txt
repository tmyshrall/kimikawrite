It came through at half past midnight, exactly as the note had warned her it would: a burst of static on the marine band, and beneath it, so faint she had to hold her breath to be sure she wasn't inventing it, a voice reading out coordinates that didn't exist on any chart she owned.

Meret wrote them down anyway. Habit, mostly. Her predecessor's logbook was full of coordinates like these, page after page, none of them ever explained, none of them ever repeating.

She looked up at the lamp, turning steady above her, throwing its long arm of light out over water that, as far as she could tell, had nothing left in it to save.

Still. The light had to turn.
