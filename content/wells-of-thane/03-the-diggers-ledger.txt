The ledger was older than the town's oldest well, its cover gone soft and dark with generations of hands. Corin found the entry by lamplight, three names back from the front page, in handwriting that wasn't quite Ada's but wasn't quite not hers either.

"Marrow Street," he read. "Dug in a dry year. Capped, not filled. See page forty for reason."

Ada took the ledger from him without a word and turned to page forty. There was no reason written there. Just a single line, underlined twice, in ink gone the color of rust:

Some wells you don't dig deeper. You dig around.
