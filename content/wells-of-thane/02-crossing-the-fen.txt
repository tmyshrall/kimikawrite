The fen road hadn't been walked in a season, and it showed. Ada went first, testing each board of the old causeway with the butt of her rod before trusting it with her weight.

Behind her, Corin carried the ledger wrapped in oilcloth against the damp. He didn't like the fen. He liked it less with the mist coming in low and slow across the reeds, swallowing the causeway a board at a time.

"You hear that?" he said.

Ada had heard it three boards back. A sound like water moving somewhere it shouldn't be moving — under the fen, not through it. She didn't stop walking. Some things you only outrun by refusing to acknowledge you've heard them.
